How Expression Trees Work

An expression tree is a binary tree representation of a mathematical expression where:

1
Leaf nodes represent operands (numbers or variables)
2
Internal nodes represent operators (+, -, *, /, ^)
3
The tree structure follows the order of operations
4
Evaluation happens from the bottom up

This visual approach helps students in rural areas understand complex mathematical concepts by breaking them down into simpler, visual components.
